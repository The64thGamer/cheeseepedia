***Bridgeville, PA (1035 Washington Pike)***, Store #535, is a corporate-owned Chuck E. Cheese's located in Chartiers Valley Shopping Center that opened in October 2002(?) with a [Studio C Beta] and still open today with the 2.0 remodel. The store is the relocation of [Bridgeville, PA (1025 Washington Pike)](/?v=cep-js&=64jti3f8sl7tpj23), which it should not be confused with.

## History

The Bridgeville Chuck E. Cheese's opened in October 2002 with [Phase 3 (2000)](/?v=cep-js&=0kupt4sbsm17vk6b) and a Studio C Beta with a [Chuck E. Cheese 32m (Animatronic)] in the Chartiers Valley Shopping Center after relocating from the [Bridgeville, PA (1025 Washington Pike)](/?v=cep-js&=64jti3f8sl7tpj23) location which was a former Pizza Time Theatre that closed with a [2-Stage](/?v=cep-js&=jh46j8lmmhexvsq9) and was located in the same plaza. The exterior remained as is, other than the letters being replaced, until the store received the 2.0 Remodel. In 2011, Bridgeville received the 2005 remodel, and in 2017, the walls were repainted to the color scheme of the 2015 remodel. On September 22, 2022, Bridgeville began its 2.0 remodel. The store would be the first in the Pittsburgh area to receive the 2021 2.0 remodel. On October 5, the Studio C Beta was removed, and on December 15, the store held its grand reopening. Chuck, Helen, Munch, and Jasper walkarounds were present at the grand reopening event.
