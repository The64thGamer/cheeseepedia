Chuck E. Cheese is a place known for creating joyful memories for families in a fun, safe, and inclusive environment. So when the Bushland High School Difference Makers - a high school community service class from Bushland, TX - needed a place to host a surprise celebration for Cari Truelock, General Manager, Kat Lopez, and her amazing team in Lubbock were honored to help.

Cari, a recipient of the national “Be a Friend Project,” which supports children who have experienced bullying, enjoyed a celebration dedicated entirely for her that was centered around love, kindness and friendship.
