A new chapter for Chuck E. Cheese International. 🇲🇽🌎

Chuck E. Cheese Adventure World is making its international debut in Jalisco, Mexico, in partnership with Grupo Dicrejart—marking the first Adventure World concept outside the United States.

Building on nearly 50 years of family entertainment experience, Chuck E. Cheese Adventure World brings together active play, games, imaginative experiences, live entertainment, great food, and family fun in an exciting new format.

This expansion marks another exciting step in the continued growth of Chuck E. Cheese internationally and demonstrates how our evolving portfolio can create new opportunities to bring the brand to families around the world.

We're excited for what's ahead in Jalisco and proud to continue growing alongside our franchise partners around the globe.
