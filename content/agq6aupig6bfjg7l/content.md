At Chuck E. Cheese, creating joyful moments and life-long memories for kids and families is what we do best.

So when a brave little boy recovering from his third open-heart surgery at Colorado Children's Hospital in Aurora had one special wish, to see his best friend Chuck E., we were honored to help make that wish come true!

Special thanks to our incredible team for helping deliver smiles, laughter, and light during this challenging time.

Field Marketing Specialist Aleesha Bishop - Colorado Springs General Manager Ciara Moore - District Manager Ryan Rodriguez - Chuck E. Cheese 🐭 🧀  

It's moments like these that remind us that the impact of what we do, and the joy of Chuck E. Cheese, extends far beyond our fun centers. We're grateful to play a small part in bringing happiness, connection, and support to families when they need it most!
