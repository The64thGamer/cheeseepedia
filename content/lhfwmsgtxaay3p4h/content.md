***Monrovia, CA (558 W Huntington Dr)* was a *Chuck E. Cheese* that opened in 2001 with a [Studio C Beta](https://cheeseepedia.org/?v=cep-solid&=6hnst5z70q7xekh9), and closed in June 2020.**

### History


Monrovia opened in 2001 as a Chuck E. Cheese's with a 16 movement Studio C Beta bot. They opened with the Major 90s Remodel, which included older decor and wall pieces. They remodeled in the early 2010s to have Avenger art. They also got a Rock Star exterior alongside getting Phase 5 posters near the front. Monrovia closed in June of 2020, due to the bankruptcy. This location might still be abandoned today.
