Adventure World is making waves, and we’re just getting started! 🎉
 
Our very own Adventure World was featured on CW33, giving viewers a look inside our three-story play destination in Arlington, TX. Kids ages 3 to 12 can run, jump, climb, and slide, parents can join in on the play, and our toddler zone with Munch’s Cosmic Playground gives the littlest explorers a space of their own. 🚀
 
And the adventure is growing! Since our first Adventure World opened in Arlington in November 2025, we’ve announced plans to bring the concept to more than 20 communities across the U.S. in 2026 and 2027, plus three locations in Jalisco, Mexico. This year, that includes West Fargo, Chandler, Rochester, Houston, Plano, and Las Vegas, with more cities coming in 2027. 🌎
 
A huge shoutout to our newly opened West Fargo, ND and Chandler, AZ Adventure Worlds, which are doing amazing. None of it happens without our incredible teams, who bring the fun to life every single day. 🧡
