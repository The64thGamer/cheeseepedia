***Amherst, NY (4994 Harlem Rd)*** was a Chuck E. Cheese's that opened on June 14, 1993, with a 1-Stage and closed on June 25, 2020, with a 1-Stage. This location should not be confused with [Amherst, NY (1551 Niagara Falls Blvd)], which was a Showbiz Pizza Place that opened on March 15, 1983, and closed in January or February 1988.

## History

The Amherst Chuck E. Cheese's opened on June 14, 1993, with the 90s remodel and a 1-Stage. It was the 13th CEC to ever open in New York and the first one to open during the 1990s in the state. In 2012, they received the 2012 update to the 2003 Cool Chuck Reconfiguration. They also received new Rockstar signage in 2013.

Rather unfortunately, the Amherst CEC became notorious for the countless violent altercations and brawls that broke out at the location. Starting in June 2015, these incidents received heavy local news coverage, tainted the store's reputation, and almost led to the store's fate being put into the town's hands. They became such a frequent issue that the local police department received around 123-126 calls for this location alone.

## Closure

The Amherst Chuck E. Cheese's permanently closed on June 25, 2020, due to the company's bankruptcy. The space is now Global Wine and Spirits.

## Fate of the Animatronics

The whereabouts of Amherst Chuck, Helen, Munch, and Jasper are unknown. Amherst Pasqually's head frame is in the possession of a private collector.
