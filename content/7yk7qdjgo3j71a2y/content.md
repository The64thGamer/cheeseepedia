***Syracuse, NY (3023 Erie Blvd)***, Store #526, is a corporate-owned Chuck E. Cheese's that opened on December 27, 2002, with a Studio C Beta and is still open with the 2.0 remodel. This store should not be confused with [Mattydale, NY (2803 Brewerton Rd)](/?v=cep-js&=ohi7vgfyntiztpn5), which was a Pizza Time Theatre that opened in August 1982 with a Balcony Stage and closed in November 1996 with a 2-Stage, and was sometimes known as "Syracuse."
