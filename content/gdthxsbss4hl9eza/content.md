Exciting news from IAAPA this week - Chuck E. Cheese is thrilled to welcome Ladybug and Cat Noir to the family as the exclusive U.S. launch partner for the first-ever Miraculous arcade game!

It's all about finding new ways to connect entertainment properties with physical experiences that make sense for the characters and their fans, and the superhero adventures kids love made this a natural fit for an arcade game - giving guests the chance to step into the action themselves.

So great to finally unveil the game alongside our partners at Miraculous Corp and Amusement Source International, and our incredible Chuck E. Cheese games team, whose expertise continues to bring exciting new experiences to our fun centers.
