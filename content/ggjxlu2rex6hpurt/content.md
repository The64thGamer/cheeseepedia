***New Hartford, NY (8555 Seneca Turnpike)*** (also known as ***Utica***) was a Pizza Time Theatre located in the [Sangertown Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangertown_Square) mall that opened on December 16, 1982, with a Balcony Stage and closed on July 29, 1985. The location was a franchise-owned location operated by Pizza Concepts, Inc. based in Rochester, NY. This store should not be confused with [New Hartford, NY (4631 Commercial Dr)], which was a Chuck E. Cheese's that opened on October 30, 2006, with a Studio C Cappa and closed on June 16, 2020.

## Pre-Opening

Prior to the opening of New Hartford, the University of Rochester invested $1.2 million in the franchising of Pizza Time Theatre stores across upstate New York, through which it received a 40% share of Rochester-based Pizza Concepts, Inc., which would later operate eight locations in New York. [1]

## History

The New Hartford Pizza Time Theater opened on December 16, 1982, in the [Sangertown Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangertown_Square) mall. The store was located right at the entrance of the mall on the left side. It opened with a [Balcony Stage] that had red and yellow/gold painted tops similar to most of the other NY stores. It's unknown exactly which guest star the store received upon opening, but it most likely had Madame Oink. It would also open with [The King / King Kat (Lounge)], with its stage notably missing [THE KING (Neon Sign)]. At some point near or shortly after its opening it would receive [Chuck E. Cheese's Cartoon Time Theatre]. Around late 1983, most likely in or around August, New Hartford would receive the [Broadway (Balcony Stage Theme)] along with Helen Henny as its guest star to go along with the [Broadway Helen Henny] showtape. In March of 1983 the Chuck and Munch walkarounds from this store would be used in a local Saint Patrick's Day parade to entertain guests and to advertise the store. [2] Also in 1983 the [The Chuck E. Cheese's Children's Choir] was established at the store. [3] Sometime before august of 1983 [THE KING (Neon Sign)] would finally be installed in the [The King / King Kat (Lounge)]. The store would also get the colorful version of the [Warblettes (Cyberamic)].

In 1984, New Hartford would give the bots on stage new outfits to go along with the [Bandstand (Balcony Stage Theme)], however they did not remove the Broadway backdrops. Later in 1984, the bots would receive the outfits for the [Country (Balcony Stage Theme)], though they were very hastily put on, with Helen's cowgirl jacket being put over her Bandstand shirt. Chuck did not receive his cowboy hat, instead keeping his standard red derby.

## Closure

On July 29, 1985, the New Hartford Pizza Time Theatre would close due poor sales and the company filing for Chapter 11 Bankruptcy the year prior. The space has been occupied by multiple businesses over the years. In 1990 the space that the Pizza Time Theatre took up was divided in half so each section could be taken up by Dentistry at Sangertown and a [RadioShack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RadioShack). [10] In March of 2019 [4] the spot was taken up by Pinz, a bowling alley and arcade bar entertainment center. In 2025, Pinz closed down, allegedly after owing hundreds of thousands of dollars in back rent. [5] As of April 2026, the site remains vacant, with the Pinz branding still visible on the outside.

## Fate of the Animatronics

When the store closed, many items were auctioned off. The King, the [Applause Board (Cyberamic)], all of the [Flag Wavers (Cyberamic)], all of the tables, and the [Chuck E. Cheese Carousel] were sold to and bought by [Enchanted Forest Water Safari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enchanted_Forest_Water_Safari) to be used in Klondike Kate's Saloon where [Rocky and the Ramblin' Rascals] are located. The King was in the back left corner of the restaurant on his own gated off stage, and the park had given him a new pair of pants. It's unknown when the King was removed. The Flag Wavers and the tables are still in Klondike Kate's Saloon, though the flags are not functional and a few of the tables' leather seats have been reupholstered.

The main 6 [Cyberamics] on stage, spare parts, tools, the walkarounds, and the [LaserDisc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaserDisc) from the game [Dragon's Lair] were loaded in a panel van by the tech team of [Colonie, NY (1440 Central Ave)]. The arcade games had already been taken out of the building by this point. [7]

During or shortly after this, the [Jasper T. Jowls (Cyberamic)] and the [Warblettes (Cyberamic)] were acquired by an employee who had planned on using them in a restuaraunt that he never opened. Jasper was supposed to be retrofitted into a rabbit, and the Warblettes country sparrows. Jasper's mask unfortunately rotted away. Jasper and The Warblettes were then sold to a collector named Felix [7] who still owns Jasper to this day, but has sold the Warblettes. The fate of the other bots as of now are unknown. [8]
