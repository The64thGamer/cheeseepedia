***New Hartford, NY (8555 Seneca Turnpike)*** (also known as ***Utica***) was a Pizza Time Theatre located in the [Sangertown Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangertown_Square) Mall that opened on December 16, 1982 with a Balcony Stage and closed on July 29, 1985. The location was a franchise-owned location operated by Pizza Concepts, Inc. based in Rochester, NY.

## Pre-Opening

Prior to the opening of New Hartford, the University of Rochester invested $1.2 million in the franchising of Pizza Time Theatre stores across upstate New York, through which it received a 40% share of Rochester-based Pizza Concepts, Inc., which would later operate eight locations in New York. [1]

## History

The New Hartford Pizza Time Theater opened on December 16th 1982 in the [Sangertown Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangertown_Square) Mall. The store was located right at the entrance of the mall on the left side.They opened with a [Balcony Stage] that had red and yellow/gold painted tops similar to most of the other NY stores. It's unknown exactly which guest star they received upon opening, but they most likely had Madame Oink.They would also open with [The King / King Kat (Lounge)] ,with their stage notably missing [THE KING (Neon Sign)] .At some point near or shortly after their opening they would receive [Chuck E. Cheese's Cartoon Time Theatre] . Around late 1983, most likely in or around August, they would receive the [Broadway (Balcony Stage Theme)] along with Helen Henny as their guest star to go along with the [Broadway Helen Henny] showtape. In March of 1983 the Chuck and Munch walkarounds from this store would be used in a local Saint Patrick's Day parade to entertain guests and to advertise the store. [2] Also in 1983 the [The Chuck E. Cheese's Children's Choir] was established at the store. [3] Sometime before august of 1983 [THE KING (Neon Sign)] would finally be installed in the [The King / King Kat (Lounge)]. The store would also get the colorful version of the [Warblettes (Cyberamic)].

In 1984 they would give the bots on stage new outfits to go along with the [Bandstand (Balcony Stage Theme)], however they did not remove the Broadway backdrops. Later in 1984 their bots would receive the outfits for the [Country (Balcony Stage Theme)] though they were very hastily put on with Helen's cowgirl jacket being put over her bandstand shirt and Chuck did not receive his cowboy hat, instead he kept the standard red derby.

## Closure

On July 29th, 1985, the store would close due poor sales and the company filing for Chapter 11 Bankruptcy the year prior. The location has been occupied by multiple businesses over the years. In 1990 the site that the Pizza Time Theatre took up was divided in half so each section could be taken up by "Dentistry at Sangertown" and a [RadioShack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RadioShack). [10] In March of 2019 [4] the spot was taken up by Pinz, a bowling alley and arcade bar entertainment center. In 2025, Pinz closed down, allegedly after owing hundreds of thousands of dollars in back rent. [5] As of April 2026, the site remains vacant with the Pinz branding still visible on the outside.

## Fate of the Animatronics

When the store closed many items were auctioned off. [The King / King Kat (Cyberamic)] , the [Applause Board (Cyberamic)], all of the [Flag Wavers (Cyberamic)], all the tables and the [Chuck E. Cheese Carousel] were sold to and bought by the [Enchanted Forest Water Safari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enchanted_Forest_Water_Safari) to be used in the Klondike Kate's Saloon where [Rocky and the Ramblin' Rascals] are located. The King was in the back left corner of the restaurant on his own gated off stage. The park had given him a new pair of pants. It's unknown when the King was removed.The [Flag Wavers (Cyberamic)] and all the tables are still in the Klondike Kate's Saloon, though the flags are not functional and a few of the table's leather seats have been reupholstered.

The main 6 [Cyberamics] on stage, spare parts, tools, the walkarounds and the [LaserDisc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaserDisc) from the game \[Dragon's Lair\]\(Dragon's Lair \(1983 video game\)\) were loaded in a panel van by the tech team of the \[Colonie\, NY \(1440 Central Ave\)\] store\. The arcade games by this point had already been taken out of the building\. \[7\]

During or shortly after this the [Jasper T. Jowls (Cyberamic)] and the [Warblettes (Cyberamic)] were acquired by an employee who had planned on using them in a restuaraunt that he never opened. Jasper was supposed to be retrofitted into a rabbit and the Warblettes were gonna be country sparrows. Jasper's mask had unfortunately rotted away. Jasper and The Warblettes were then sold to a collector named Felix [7] who still owns Jasper to this day, but has sold the Warblettes. The fate of the other bots as of now are unknown. [8]
