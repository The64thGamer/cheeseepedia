***Colonie, NY (1440 Central Ave*)** (also known as ***Albany***) was a Chuck E. Cheese's Pizza Time Theatre that opened in 1982 with a Balcony Stage and closed on September 10th, 1995, with a Chuck E.'s House Facade.

## History

The Colonie PTT opened in 1982 with a Balcony Stage with the World of Music theme and Madame Oink. The store was one of the first five Pizza Time Theatre locations to open in the state of New York. In 1989, the store received a Chuck E.'s House Facade, which would remain at the store until its closure.

## Closure

On November 10, 1995, the Colonie store closed. It is speculated that the store relocated to [Latham, NY (601 Troy-Schenectady Rd)], but this is not confirmed.
