***1270-1272 El Camino Real, Store #340*** is a Chuck E. Cheese that opened on May 30, 1994 with a [1-Stage], and is still open to this day with the [CEC 2.0 Remodel Program].

## History

San Bruno opened on May 30, 1994 with the SPT 1992/1993 Remodel Program and [1-Stage]. We don’t know when they received the [Phase 1 (1994)], [Phase 2 (1997)], and [Phase 3 (2000)] game packages, but they probably receive all of them at one point. In 2007, San Bruno would receive the [CEC 2000's Remodel Program]. In 2019, the location would receive the [CEC 2.0 Remodel Program], removing the 1-Stage for a 2019 Model[Chuck E. Live Stage (Dance Floor)]. As of September 2023, San Bruno still has the 2.0 remodel.
