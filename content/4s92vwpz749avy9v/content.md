Wishing a joyful and colorful Holi to all who celebrate!

Also known as the Festival of Colors, Holi is celebrated by many in the Hindu community as a joyful welcome to spring and a celebration of the triumph of good over evil. This vibrant holiday is marked by the throwing of gulal (colored powders), festive bonfires, dancing, traditional delicacies such as gujiya and thandai.
