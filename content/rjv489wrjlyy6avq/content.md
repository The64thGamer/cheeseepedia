***Buffalo, NY (2638 Delaware Ave)***, officially known as ***North Buffalo***, is a Chuck E. Cheese's that opened on November 22, 2024, with a [Chuck E. Live Stage] and is still open. This store should not be confused with [Hamburg, NY (4408 Milestrip Rd)], which is a Chuck E. Cheese's that opened on May 31, 2000, with a [Studio C Alpha] and is still open with the 2.0 remodel, and is commonly known as "Buffalo."
