***New Hartford, NY (4631 Commercial Dr)***, Store #463, was a corporate-owned Chuck E. Cheese's that opened on October 30, 2006, with a [Studio C Cappa](/?v=cep-js&=8yfyq2z7qgz9mz9i) and closed on June 16, 2020, during a wave of closures due to the company's bankruptcy. This location should not be confused with [New Hartford, NY (8555 Seneca Turnpike)], which was a Chuck E. Cheese's Pizza Time Theatre that opened on December 16, 1982, with a Balcony Stage and closed on July 29, 1985.

## History

The New Hartford Chuck E. Cheese's opened on October 30, 2006, with the [CEC 2000's Remodel Program] and a [Studio C Cappa] V3 with a [Chuck E. Cheese 16m (Animatronic)]. At some point in the store's life, Chuck's hat and, oddly, his eyebrows were removed.

## Closure

On June 16, 2020, the New Hartford Chuck E. Cheese's permanently closed due to the company's bankruptcy. It was one of several stores to close because of this. The site is now Mr. Kimchi.

## Fate of the Animatronic

New Hartford's 16m Chuck bot partially survived; these parts are now owned by a private collector. The rest of the animatronic was destroyed.
