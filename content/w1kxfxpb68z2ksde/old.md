***2239 148th Ave*** is a *Chuck E. Cheese's* that opened around February 1996 (day unknown) and closed in April 27th, 2025 with a [1-Stage].

## Stage

Bellevue opened with a [1-Stage],. It is rumored this stage came from the now closed [Milwaukee, WI (7401 Good Hope Rd)] store. They at first had the tuxedo outfit, later the Cool Chuck outfit. The curtains were disabled sometime in the 2005 time period, and later completely removed all together. Chuck E. later got the Avenger outfit, removing the cap by the end of 2013.

## History

Bellevue opened sometime in or around February 1996 (day unknown). with a [1-Stage] and the [Phase 1 (1994)] remodel. Unfortunately, there isn't much info on Bellevue from around this time. In 2008/2009, Bellevue received the 2005 update to the 2003 'Cool Chuck' Reconfiguration / Major Remodel Initiative, which involved the removal of 90s decor, showroom walls, balcony seating, and the addition of purple walls and an exterior change. As of April 27, Bellevue has closed.
