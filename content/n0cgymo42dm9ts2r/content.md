***West Islip, NY (155 Sunrise Hwy)***, Store #511, is a corporate-owned Chuck E. Cheese's that opened on December 10, 1998, with a [Studio C Alpha](/?v=cep-js&=y3u8rr4k5280egzq) and is still open today with the 2.0 remodel.

## Stage

The West Islip location opened with a [Studio C Alpha](/?v=cep-js&=y3u8rr4k5280egzq) in 1998. as of July 1st 2024 the Stage was removed and Destroyed.

## History

The West Islip Chuck E. Cheese's opened on December 10, 1998, with [Phase 3 (1997)] and a Studio C Alpha with a [Chuck E. Cheese 32m (Animatronic)]. In the late 2000s, the store received the [CEC 2000's Remodel Program](/?v=cep-js&=gwji4ow3ao6sc3zq). It is unknown if the location previously had showroom walls, which would have been removed. The exterior was unchanged after the 2005 remodel, sporting the original logo from its opening. The exterior was refreshed in 2016 due to a minor plaza remodel, aligned to that of the CEC 2015 Update Program. On July 1, 2024, the store underwent the [CEC 2.0 Remodel Program](/?v=cep-js&=27e9rtfjazslj9uz), and on that day, the Studio C Alpha was removed and destroyed.
