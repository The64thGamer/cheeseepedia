Chuck E. Cheese is THE champion of childhood fun, and play is the foundation. 🎉

This May, we're also champions of mental health awareness!

For nearly 50 years, Chuck E. Cheese has been the place where kids come to laugh, move, explore, and just be kids. This Mental Health Awareness Month, we're proud to put that purpose to work as part of the MESH Helps, Inc. Play for Resilience initiative. From May 1–8, families visiting more than 450 Chuck E. Cheese locations across the U.S. will experience firsthand how play supports social-emotional development, with curated video messaging featured on screens every hour highlighting the connection between play and resilience in kids.

Through active play, family-friendly arcade games, and loveable characters, Chuck E. Cheese celebrates play not just as fun, but as a form of resilience.
MESH Helps will connect families with free resources that offer:

•Solutions and conversation-starters for caregivers
•Mind-stimulating activities that encourage healthy play
•Tools to support emotional growth, problem-solving, and connection

Every kid deserves a place to play, grow, and thrive, and Chuck E. Cheese is proud to be that place!
