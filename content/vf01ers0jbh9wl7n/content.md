Chet Fattykins is one of the members of the 4 Seasoned Bears, from the **[Bear Country Jubilee]** show created by Creative Engineering. He is a parody of the real life musician [Chet Atkins.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chet_Atkins)

### **History**

He would appear on the **[Bear Country Jubilee]** shows sold to [Morey's Pier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morey%27s_Piers) and [Americana Amusement Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LeSourdsville_Lake_Amusement_Park) along with the rest of the 4 Seasoned Bears on Center Stage.
When the **Country Bear Jubilee** showtape was released, he would be replaced by **[Billy Wilbur]**, but his character design would still be used for Billy Wilbur with minor changes at [Morey's Pier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morey%27s_Piers) and [Americana Amusement Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LeSourdsville_Lake_Amusement_Park).

### **Personality**

Not much is currently known about him, as the Bear Country Jubilee showtape is still lost media as of writing this.
