***York, PA (2801 E Market St)***, Store #965, is a Chuck E. Cheese located at the York Mall that opened on February 28, 1983, as a Showbiz Pizza Place with the Rock-afire Explosion, and received Concept Unification in 1991. The store is still open with the 2.0 remodel. It is not to be confused with [York, PA (351 Loucks Rd)](/?v=cep-js&=lpscpj6jsrai7nnv), which was a Pizza Time Theatre that opened on August 23, 1983, and closed on February 19, 1984.

## History

### Pre-Showbiz

Prior to becoming Showbiz Pizza Place, the space in which the restaurant would be located was an A&P grocery store; about 10,000 square feet of the 22,000 total square feet would be taken up by Showbiz [3]. The first mentions of Showbiz taking over the old store came in July 1982 [1], and the original opening date of the restaurant was set for December 1, but this was pushed back for unknown reasons [2].

### Opening

The York Showbiz Pizza Place soft-opened on February 28, 1983 [1], but officially opened on March 4 at 11:00am, and employed 75 people [4]. Franchised by Family Restaurant Concepts, Inc. [1], York was the 158th Showbiz to ever open [4]. York's Rock-afire bots were the 22nd batch of July 1982.

### Brief Closure

On October 13, 1989, the restaurant closed to do its franchisee filing for bankruptcy. In November, McBiz Corporation purchased the store, and it reopened on December 6. [1]

### Concept Unification

During the week of August 6-12, 1990, the York Showbiz Pizza Place underwent Concept Unification. The store's manager was Bill Hutchins at the time. [5]

### Post-CU & Present Day

For the rest of its time in the store, York's 3-Stage remained in great condition. In September 2018, the store received the 2.0 remodel. [1] At the time of the remodel, the 3-Stage animatronics (with the exception of the Wink) had been in the store for 35 years. Their fates are unknown. Additionally, the store is now owned by corporate.
