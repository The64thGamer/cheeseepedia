We are proud to announce that Randy Forsythe has been named President & Chief Operating Officer of CEC Entertainment. 🎉
 
Randy's journey with our company began in 1982, and over more than four decades he has become one of the true architects of who we are today. From Regional Vice President across multiple regions to countless other leadership roles along the way, Randy has helped grow our footprint from 37 restaurants to more than 500 locations worldwide.
 
In his expanded role, Randy will lead Field Operations, Global Sourcing and Purchasing, the Games Department, Construction, Learning & Development, Marketing, Brand Licensing, Information Technology, International Franchise, as well as the Peter Piper Pizza business — bringing his signature dedication and deep institutional knowledge to an even broader part of our business.
 
What has always set Randy apart is not just what he has built, but how he has built it. With heart, consistency, and an unmatched understanding of this company and the people who make it run.
 
Congratulations, Randy. We could not be prouder to celebrate this milestone. 🧡
