The ***Fatz Geronimo Mijjin ***was an animatronic produced by [Creative Engineering] for the [New Rock-afire Explosion] show in 1993 [1] He is the successor to the [Fatz Geronimo (Early Mijjin)] built the year prior for [Creative Engineering] 's show [The Mijjins]

## History

The Fatz Geronimo Mijjin would start development and be finnished in 1993 [2] The bot is an improved version of the [Fatz Geronimo (Early Mijjin)] built the year prior,with the major diffrences being the bot having a standard Mijjin mech and various cosmetic diffrences,mainly in the tuxedo.

The first 2 [New Rock-afire Explosion] shows built known as the "beta" shows had multiple minor cosmetic and scenic differences from later shows.The only major diffrence on Fatz being the color of the buttons on his Tuxedo.Instead of the typical orange buttons,the first show,initially used as the proggraming stage but then shipped to [Pleasure Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleasure_Island_Family_Theme_Park) had black buttons.The second show,used on [Creative Engineering] 's programing stage and built right after the first show was shipped out had white buttons. [3]

The bot would continue to be installed at multiple Family Entertainment Centers and theme parks throughout the 90's with the last 2 installed publicly at the 2 Odyssey fun world locations being removed in January of 2019,and are now owned by private collectors. [7]

## Design

This bot is a standard Mijjin mech with brown eye pieces.The bot is a smaller Mijjin style version of the [Fatz Geronimo (Animatronic)] from the [Rock-afire Explosion] sporting the classic Gold tuxedo.The bowtie is made with a Sparkly Rainbow fabric with Black dots. The same fabric was used for Mitzi's Dress.

## Mechanical Structure

The [Mijjins] are mostly made up of a casted resin/plastic parts,with a few square aluminium bars,steal rods,steal universal joints,and varios nuts,bolts and washers of various sizes.The head of the character also incluedes 2 custom eyes and a pair of [Rock-afire Explosion] eylids connected togheter from the back.On top of the mech goes over multiple vacuum formed transparent plastic shells that give the character shape.Then on top of those go the leather guards that protect the cosmetics from getting caught in the mech trough the gaps in the shells.The bot is mounted onto a circular wooden base with smiley face-esc cutouts on the top.The "eyes" are where the character's feet go while the mouth is a trapdoor which reveals the character's valves,on the bottom of the door is also put achart with the character's movements and valve numbers [9] 

The character like almost every other [Creative Engineering] bot runs on pneumatic cylinders produced by the companies **Fabco-Air Inc** and custom **Creative Engineering.inc** branded cylinders.These cylinders are than actuated with **Mac valves** stored in the character's circular base.The eye movements are controlled with servos,the brand of these servos is unknown.
