Chuck E. spent his Halloween weekend on the trail! 🎃🧡

Our Chuck E. Cheese team in Burnsville, MN joined the 44th annual Rosemount Halloween Haunted Trail, a family-friendly community tradition. We handed out candy to trail walkers, and Chuck E. met friends all night long. 👻🍬

Moments like these are what community is all about, and we’re so proud of the team who showed up to make them happen.

Shoutout to Hafsa Idris and the Burnsville team members who made it happen!
