At Chuck E. Cheese, creating positive, long-lasting memories for families is our specialty.

Recently in Bradenton, FL, we had the privilege of hosting a Make-A-Wish send-off party for an incredible young guest, Nathan, as he prepares for an unforgettable trip to SeaWorld and Universal.

Thanks to the thoughtful coordination and care of General Manager Kyle Martin and District Manager Seneca Gray Robinson, Nathan's visit was filled with his favorite games, delicious pizza, and a special meet & greet with Chuck E. Cheese! 

We’re proud to play a small role in backdropping moments like these, where joy, community, and celebration come together in the most meaningful way.
