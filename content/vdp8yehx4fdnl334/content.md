The ***Buddy Crash (Animatronic)*** was an animatronic character built by [Creative Engineering] for the [Bear Country Jubilee] which was sold to Morey's Piers and Americana Amusement Park.

## History

The first Buddy Crash bot started development and was built in late 1977-early 1978 along with the rest of the [Bear Country Jubilee] after being commissioned by the [LeSourdsville Lake Amusement Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LeSourdsville_Lake_Amusement_Park) which bough it as part of a 3.5 million dollar expansion and rebranding to become the "*Americana Amusement Park - the Great American Amusement Park"*. [2]
Buddy would be scheduled to be installed in the spring of 1978 along with the rest of the show. [3] This first bot would operate for 16 years until the show's removal in 1993.It is unknown what happened to this bot

Shortly after the Americana show was installed, [Morey's Pier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morey%27s_Piers) commissioned a [Bear Country Jubilee] of their own from [Creative Engineering] .With this came a second Buddy Crash bot which would be installed in the same year.This bot would operate for 6 years,time in which the show fell into major disrepair in its final years.At the end of the 1984 operating season [1] the entirety of the *Bear Country Jubilee was sold back to* [Creative Engineering] .When the Buddy Crash from this set arrived he was relatively beat up,with his mask melting,especially near the eye sockets [4] The entire show would be restored in 1984 bringing Buddy back to his former glory.The whole show was also upgraded to a modified [Rock-afire Explosion Control System] instead of the original yet by then outdated [Tone Decoder Control System].His original base was also replaced with the base of a Dook Larue.The show was intended to be sold however nobody ended up buying it.Eventually around 1987 the show was moved in the space [6] that once was the first programing room [7] at [Creative Engineering] .The bot stood there for many years until late 2023 when the Jefferson building would be emptied out to move the assets to the [Creative Engineering Museum]

The bot currently resides right in front of the Center stage of the [Classic Stage] at the museum.

It's important to note both of these bots only acted as Buddy Crash for a very short time.When [Creative Engineering] introduced the successor to the [Bear Country Jubilee] at the [CEI 1979 IAAPA Show (Event)] named the [Hard Luck Bears] the original [Bear Country Jubilee Showtape] was replaced at both Americana [8] and Morey's Pier with a slightly different version of the showtape used for the [Hard Luck Bears] .It's unclear if this version of the tape or the one used for the [Hard Luck Bears] is the original version,but the [Bear Country Jubilee] version (used at Americana) has multiple noticeable differences,such as a short segment at the beginning for [Mortimer Moose] and [Buford Buffalo] ,the complete absence of [Gooney Bird] , [Repete Thornsberry] 's singing actually being heard during the chorus of the Smitty's song,and varios other minor differences like certain segments,specifically the ones where [Gooney Bird] speaks in the [Hard Luck Bears] version the having noticeable background noise,implying the clips could be uncut.

In this version of the tape Buddy took on the role of [Billy Boy]

## Design

He is a skinny black bear with a tan face,long snout and brown eyes

#### **Americana v1**

When this first bot was initially installed he wore a black top hat and a blue vest with golden accents

#### **Morey's Pier**

Morey's was given a pair of dark blue overalls,a large red bandana and a large straw hat.

#### **Americana v2**

At some point the Americana bot was given a red bandana and the vest was removed

## Drums

#### **Creative Engineering**

When Buddy was completed and photographed at CEI his drums were much simpler and incomplete looking,with him having one plain white bass,one white mouned tom and a silver snare

#### **Americana**

Budy at Americana Buddy stood on a large rectangular riser with fake grass all around,he had a set of silver drums concisting of a bass,snare and mounted tom.The bass had a custom painted front,reading **FOUR SEASONED BEARS** in dark blue tilted letters and a green moonshine jug with a white label showing 3 black X's and blue highlights on the top and bottom.Around the jug are 3 blue puttles and 4 bubbles coming out the top with 2 more popped bublles

#### **Morey's Pier**

At Morey's Buddy stood on a large rectangular riser with fake grass all around, had a set of sparkly blue drums concisting of a bass,snare and mounted tom.The bass had a custom painted front,reading **FOUR SEASONED BEARS**,with the "SEASONED BEARS" part being made up of dark blue letters,the "THE" being black and tilted,and the "4" being yellow with an orange dropshadow.Ther e are 2 green moonshine jugs with a white label showing 3 black X's and blue highlights on the top and bottom.Around the jugs there are 3 blue puttles and 6 bubbles coming out the top with 2 more popped bublles

#### **Creative Engineering 1984**

Buddy was taken off his original riser and instead put on a Dook Larue riser.He kept everything from his Morey's drum set but he was also given a Cymbal.

## Movements

The bot has 6 movements.On the outside of the bot only 5 of the valves are visible as the valve for the mouth is inside the bot's body
While each movement has it's own valve certain movements on the show run off of the same bit meaning they'd be synced up.However it's not clear which of these movements were synced as the show's [Bit Chart] as of now is unknown.
