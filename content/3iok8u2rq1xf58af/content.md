The ***After Christmas 1983 Showtape ***was a Rock-afire Explosion showtape released in ShowBiz Pizza Place locations in December 1983. The tape is an altered version of the Christmas 1983 Showtape with all Christmas-themed songs removed and includes Get Ready, Red Hot as a substitute.
