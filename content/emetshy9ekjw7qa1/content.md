We have some exciting news to share! 🎉
 
Please join us in congratulating Randy Forsythe on his promotion to President & Chief Operating Officer of CEC Entertainment! In this expanded role, Randy will lead Field Operations, Global Sourcing and Purchasing, the Games Department, Construction, Learning & Development, Marketing, Brand Licensing, Information Technology, International Franchise, as well as the Peter Piper Pizza business.
 
Randy's story is one for the books. He first walked through our doors in 1982 when the company was still known as Showbiz Pizza, and he has been a part of our family ever since. When he started, we had 37 restaurants. Today, we have more than 500 locations around the world — and Randy's fingerprints are on so much of that growth.
 
Over more than four decades, Randy has held roles across the company that have helped shape the Chuck E. Cheese experience that families know and love today. He has always said that people are his greatest asset, and that the relationships built across this company are like family. That philosophy shows in everything he does.
 
Congratulations, Randy. Here's to the next chapter! 🧡
