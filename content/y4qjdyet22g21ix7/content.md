We're back with our Intern Spotlight! 🌟

At CEC Entertainment, an internship is more than a line on a resume. It's where careers begin, communities are built, and people find their place. 🧡

This week we are spotlighting four incredible members of our intern family, and one who never really left.

Meet Tyson Gardner, Kennedi Foreman, Rachel Chavarin, Lila, and Becka!

💻 Tyson hit the ground running with his biggest project yet — tracking down the source of targeted scams on our employees and helping put a stop to them. He loves that executives are approachable, everyone is helpful, and highlighted his manager, Adam Sullivan, for being down to earth, making introductions, and giving Tyson the space to shine.

🤝 Kennedi loves that the little wins get celebrated just as much as the big ones here. She highlighted the sense of community, appreciates never feeling intimidated asking questions, and loves that hard work and fun coexist at CEC.

🌱 Rachel has grown her communication skills working closely alongside her co-workers Perla Castillo and MacKayla Harris and discovered a future career goal of becoming a Benefits Analyst along the way. She also loved getting to make her own pizza in store and see how our fun centers operate firsthand.

👥 Lila loves that nobody is ever just doing one thing here. She highlights a culture where everyone wears many hats, pitches in where needed, and shows up happy to do it.

✨ And then there is Becka, our full circle moment. She started at the store level, worked her way up to an Intern, and currently works as an HR I-9 Administrator. 20 years never flew by faster! Her highlights have been the people that show genuine care, building family bonds with her teams, and the leaders that believe in you and push you to keep growing. She loves that every day is different and that at Chuck E. Cheese, everyone is welcomed and celebrated.

Our interns are not just passing through. They are part of something that lasts. And for some, this is only the beginning. 🌟
