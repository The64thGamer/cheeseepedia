The Peter Piper Pizza team is coming together this season to support Phoenix Children's Toyland, helping bring holiday cheer to patients and their families.

Guests can make a donation at any Phoenix Peter Piper Pizza location through Dec. 16, and we’ll match donations up to $2,500. Plus, our team members will volunteer at the hospital’s Toy Vault to help ensure toys reach children in time for the holidays.

We’re proud to play a small role in supporting our community during this meaningful time of year!
