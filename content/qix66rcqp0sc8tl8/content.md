Loved this tape. Feels right at home with the ShowBiz Klunk style but a bit toned down (in a good way to not be annoying). Had some genuine laughs with the Apple stock and 'Top of his class' lines. Glad to see that writing style can be preserved 40 years later without pandering or changing too much, hope this doesn't have to be a one time thing and can continue on! Things always get good once you've got a few complete works under your belt and can really hone the craft, we need more Klunk!

I will say going for the ShowBiz Klunk with Bob Pondillo over Jeff Howell/Shawn Fernandez wouldn't be my personal pick, but of course absolutely amazing to have him reprise his role! I guess I had a sweet spot for the more laid back Klunk, and assuming if Aaron ever went to make a new show he was gonna keep his original vision for the character. I mean technically Aaron did not produce this whole tape so maybe that's the case! I'd be down for two completely different Klunk style tapes going forward, Jack and Bob doing ShowBiz style ones and Aaron doing ones closer to the Jeff Howell one with whatever VA would take that role. A girl can dream oh well.

I think it was a bit jarring seeing Murray be a secondary role rather than a lead-in to the wacky phone calls, again if we get more tapes I hope he's paired back again and a wider variety of crazy calls are focused on, that was the fun of the ShowBiz skits! Regardless, great performance and kudos to everyone on the team that made that happen.
