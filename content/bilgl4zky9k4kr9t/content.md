***The Uncle Klunk Abomination: The Return*** is the fourth [Uncle Klunk] showtape, released on November 21st, 2026 as a collaboration between [Creative Engineering], Jack Turner, and Sully G, with the original ShowBiz Klunk voice actor, Bob Pondillo, reprising his role.[1]
