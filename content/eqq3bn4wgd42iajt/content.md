***Night Shift at Chuck E. Cheese's 2*** was a [Chuck E. Cheese's] fan-game on Roblox inspired by [Five Nights at Freddy's] which released on February 19th, 2024. The game would gain popularity throughout Summer of 2025 and would eventually receive a cease and desist by CEC Entertainment on August 29th, 2025 and was taken down. The game would rebrand as "Night Shift Legacy" and "Night Shift at Paulie's" with reskins of the characters.
