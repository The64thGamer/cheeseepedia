Peter Piper Pizza's Summer Pass is back ☀️ and we’re loving the excitement so far!
 
Available through August 2, the Summer Pass gives families two months of unlimited visits, daily game play and expanded savings on food, drinks and active play, making summer fun more accessible for families in Phoenix, Tucson, Albuquerque and National City, CA.
 
Pass options include:
• Fun Pass: 40 daily game points + 20% off active play and select food and drinks
• Big Fun Pass: 110 daily game points + 30% off active play and select food and drinks
• Mega Fun Pass: 250 daily game points + 50% off active play and select food and drinks
