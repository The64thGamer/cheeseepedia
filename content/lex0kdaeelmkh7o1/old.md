***650 Bald Hill Rd, Store #645*** is a *Chuck E. Cheese's* located in the Midland Commons Shopping Center in Warwick, Rhode Island, as a freestanding building. It opened on March 6, 2000, with a [Studio C Alpha]. This is not to be confused with the [Warwick, RI (1960 Post Rd)] location, which opened in June 1984 and closed in Late 1990. It then relocated to [Warwick, RI (1245 Bald Hill Rd)], opening in Late 1990, and closed in November 19, 1994.

## History

The location opened March 6, 2000 in a freestanding building just outside the Rhode Island Mall (Now Midland Commons Shopping Center) with the [SPT 1990's Remodel Program] as the first in CEC Rhode Island since the closure of [Warwick, RI (1245 Bald Hill Rd)]. This would be the first location in New England to receive any kind of Studio C stage. On October 1, 2012 Warwick begin the [CEC 2000's Remodel Program]. During the remodel, all aspects of previous remodels would be removed. Being replaced with the standard by 2012.(1) Between April-May 2017, the Skytubes would be removed, Between July-August, Tokens would be replaced with [Chuck E's Play Pass]. On April 27, 2024, the Trampoline Zone was installed.(2) On July 8, 2024, the store would begin its [CEC 2.0 Remodel Program], removing the stage for a [Video Wall] and [Chuck E. Live Stage (Dance Floor)].

## Stages

The store would open March 6, 2000 debuting with a [Studio C Alpha]. The Late Night Outfit would be removed and replaced with the Avenger outfit on April 30, 2011. In October 2012, the [Interactive Console] and Bluescreen would be removed and replaced with CEC Panel and Ticket Blaster backdrop respectively due to [CEC 2000's Remodel Program]. Chuck's cap would be removed in September 2013. and in July 2014 the curtains would be disabled. On July 8, 2024, the [Studio C Alpha] would be removed in favor of the [Video Wall] and [Chuck E. Live Stage (Dance Floor)] as a part of its [CEC 2.0 Remodel Program]. Warwick was the last location to have the rubber eyelashes, excluding Corporate.
