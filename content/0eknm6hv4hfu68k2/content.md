As Ramadan begins, we recognize this important month observed by Muslims around the globe. Ramadan, the ninth month of the Islamic calendar, is marked by daily fasting from dawn to sunset, increased prayer, reflection, and acts of generosity. It is a time focused on spiritual growth, self-discipline, and community under the Five Pillars of Islam.

At Chuck E. Cheese, we value the diverse traditions and experiences that enrich our teams. We encourage everyone to take time to learn more about Ramadan, be mindful of colleagues who may be fasting, and offer understanding and support throughout the month.

Wishing all who observe a meaningful and peaceful Ramadan.
