***Monroeville, PA (3813 William Penn Hwy)*** was a *Pizza Time Theatre* that opened on March 1982 with a [Balcony Stage], and relocated across the street to its [Monroeville, PA (3800 William Penn Hwy)] in March 1993 with a [Chuck E.'s House Facade (C-Stage)]. This location was the first Pizza Time Theatre/Chuck E. Cheese to open in Pennsylvania.
