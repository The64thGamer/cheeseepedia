***Danbury, CT (20 Backus Ave)***, Store #3231, was a Chuck E. Cheese's located in the Airport Plaza. that opened on March 1, 2010 with a Studio C Beta, and closed on August 24, 2013. This store should not be confused with [Danbury, CT (26 Backus Ave)] which was a Discovery Zone.

## History

### Pre-CEC

Before Chuck E. Cheese's opened, a club occupied the space.

### Years of Operation

The Danbury Chuck E. Cheese's opened on March 1, 2010, with a Studio C Beta with a 16-movement Chuck bot that donned the Avenger outfit. The store quickly gained a poor reputation not long after opening, as maintenance was neglected, especially when it came to the stage. By 2011, the stage was already having issues with the lighting [1][2] and show DVDs [1]. By 2012, Chuck's condition had noticeably worsened, with there being "tons of air leaks" and his clothes being dirty [3]. Aside from the show, the store itself was also lacking; some customers reported in poor reviews that the food was "virtually inedible," and the staff was "often too busy playing the games themselves to help the kids," and one individual commented that "It was having issues with the show, and the store in general was disgusting...They would've kept the store open if it was clean and there was a fair amount of popularity. Obviously this store didn't have it and it just wasn't going to get any better" [4].

### Closure

On August 24, 2013, the Danbury Chuck E. Cheese's permanently closed without any warning being given to customers. The store closed due to its poor maintenance and customer service, and negative reviews from customers. The building remained vacant for about a year until a Bev-Max opened in the space. Later on, a SportClips opened in the space.

### Fate of the Animatronics

After the store closed, the Chuck bot and Pizza Time Clock were sent to [Wilmington, NC (4389 Oleander Dr)](/?v=cep-js&=4i02eene1q80pu17), but remained in storage and went unused. After Wilmington closed on October 13, 2020, Chuck was destroyed, but the Clock was saved by a private collector.
