***Chuck E. Cheese Pizza*** is a fast food mall chain started by an unknown Chilean franchisee of [Chuck E. Cheese's] in late October of 2026.[3][4]

## History

The first location to open would be in Mall Plaza Los Dominicos between October 23rd to October 31st, 2026.[1][2][3] Prior to opening, the construction walls would be marked with "Chuck E. Cheese Pronto".
