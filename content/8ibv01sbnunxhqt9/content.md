***Chuck E. Cheese Pizza*** is a fast food mall chain started by HPDM Entertainment, a Chilean franchisee of [Chuck E. Cheese's], on October 31st, 2026.[3][4]

## History

The first location to open would be in [Santiago, CL (Mallplaza Los Dominicos) (Padre Hurtado Sur 875)] on October 31st, 2026.[1][2][3] Prior to opening, the construction walls would be marked with "Chuck E. Cheese Pronto". The location would feature a food ordering counter with arcade machines in a sectioned off area, with another section in the food court seating area dubbed "Chuck E. Cheese Arcade Zone" with more machines. In total 15 machines would be available at opening. [6] The food stand also features a mini prize corner, alongside a 'Chuck E. Box'. [5] It is unclear if the name is a homage to the prior name of [Chuck E. Cheese's] restaurants, being "Chuck E. Cheese's Pizza".

On the 25th of November, José Luis Lagos, CEO of HPDM Entertainment, would comment on the location in the first public acknowledgement:

> “This venue number 13, which is the pizzeria, is a new format that comes to take the brand to places where we were not 100% present and that we knew was wanted by our customers. There we invented this format knowing that pizza is something that people have always valued a lot, not only in Chile, but internationally...” “...is born from the people’s request. There I saw the opportunity and said, ‘People are talking, let’s hear it,’...” "...The new Chuck & Cheese format is undoubtedly another reason to visit this urban center...”
