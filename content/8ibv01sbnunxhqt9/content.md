***Chuck E. Cheese Pizza*** is a fast food mall chain started by an unknown Chilean franchisee of [Chuck E. Cheese's] on October 31st, 2026.[3][4]

## History

The first location to open would be in [Santiago, CL (Mallplaza Los Dominicos) (Padre Hurtado Sur 875)] on October 31st, 2026.[1][2][3] Prior to opening, the construction walls would be marked with "Chuck E. Cheese Pronto". The location would feature a small food ordering counter with a couple arcade machines next to it.
