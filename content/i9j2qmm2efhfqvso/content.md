***Levittown, NY (3046 Hempstead Turnpike)*** was a Chuck E. Cheese's that opened in January 1985 with a [Balcony Stage] and closed in 1989. This location was the very first "modern" CEC to open.

## History

The Levittown Chuck E. Cheese's opened in January 1985 with a Balcony Stage, which was the fourth-to-last to ever be installed. Levittown was the first "modern" Chuck E. Cheese's to open, as well as the first of just three locations to open in 1985.

### Closure

In 1989, the Levittown Chuck E. Cheese's closed due to unknown reasons. Over the years, the building became several different businesses before it was demolished for Miller's Ale House in 2007, which remains on the site.
