Chuck E. Cheese Adventure World to make international debut in Mexico

Chuck E. Cheese, the US-headquartered family entertainment brand, has entered into an agreement with Grupo Dicrejart to introduce its new Chuck E. Cheese Adventure World concept in Jalisco, Mexico.

Chuck E. Cheese Adventure World combines active play, games, dedicated areas for younger children and live entertainment, alongside the Kid Check safety programme.
