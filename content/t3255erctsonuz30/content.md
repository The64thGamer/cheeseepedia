Cooper and Ryder spent a fun-filled day at Chuck E. Cheese Adventure World in Arlington, and we’re so grateful we got to be part of it.

These brothers, ages 5 and 1, are in foster care and waiting for their forever family. They already love Chuck E. Cheese, so when the Heart Gallery of North Texas asked us to host them for their WFAA Wednesday’s Child feature, we jumped at the chance. They spent the day exploring, playing and just being kids.

Thank you to WFAA and the Heart Gallery of North Texas from the Gladney Center for Adoption. If you or someone you know could be the family Cooper and Ryder are waiting for, watch their segment and learn more: https://lnkd.in/g5Y4CsMN
