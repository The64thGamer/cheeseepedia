Supporting the communities we serve has always been core to who we are at Peter Piper Pizza. When food brings people together, it creates moments that truly matter.

That is why our heart-shaped pizza campaign is especially meaningful here in Arizona. Our local teams are building on last year’s momentum with the goal of raising even more for Children's Miracle Network Network with an all-new offer.

Alongside our heart-shaped pizzas, we're offering the Sweetheart Special through Feb. 14 at our Arizona locations only. Guests can enjoy the Sweetheart Special which includes a large pepperoni pizza and choice of a regular OREO® Crunch Dessert or Cinnamon Crunch Dessert for $22.99, with $1 from each purchase also benefiting Children's Miracle Network.

We are grateful to be part of communities that show up, give back and support one another, one shared meal at a time. ❤️
