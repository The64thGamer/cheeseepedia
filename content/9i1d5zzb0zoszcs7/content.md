***Amherst, NY (1551 Niagara Falls Blvd)*** was a Showbiz Pizza Place that opened on March 15, 1983, with a Classic Stage, and closed in either January or February 1988 with a Classic Stage. The store reopened as a Major Magic's All-Star Pizza Revue in 1988 and closed by the mid-1990s.

## Showbiz Pizza Place

The Amherst Showbiz Pizza Place opened on March 15, 1983 [1], as the first of two SPPs in New York. The store was franchised by Lawrence, Kansas-based Family Restaurant Concepts, Inc. and at the time of its opening employed 90 full-time and part-time employees and contained 70 games and rides alongside a wide-screen TV [1].

Due to their greater popularity and profits, both Showbiz stores outcompeted the nearby Pizza Time Theatre at the Thruway Mall in Cheektowaga. After the PTT closed on July 29, 1985, the stores allowed former Chuck E. Cheese's customers to exchange their tokens for Showbiz tokens [3].

In either January or February of 1988, both the Amherst and West Seneca Showbiz stores were shuttered.
