***Greece, NY (3160 W Ridge Rd)*** was a Chuck E. Cheese's Pizza Time Theatre located within what is now Lowe's Plaza that opened on March 15, 1983, with a Balcony Stage and closed in November 1996 with a 2-Stage. This location should not be confused with [Greece, NY (3130 W Ridge Rd)], which is a Chuck E. Cheese's that opened on July 5, 2003, in the same plaza with a Studio C Beta and is still open today with the 2.0 remodel.

## History

The Greece Pizza Time Theatre opened on March 15, 1983. Its opening made it the second Pizza Time Theatre in the Rochester area after [Penfield, NY (1614 Penfield Rd)], as well as the sixth in New York. Greece's Balcony Stage opened with the [Tunnel of Love (Balcony Stage Theme)] that featured Madame Oink. By August 1983, Greece would have Helen Henny, and would get the [Broadway (Balcony Stage Theme)] as a result of this. Greece also opened with The King, who was in the store for its entire lifetime. In 1989, the store would have its Balcony Stage retrofitted into a Chuck E.'s House Facade. In 1992, the store would receive its final stage, a 2-Stage.

## Closure and Fate of the Animatronics

In November 1996, the Greece Chuck E. Cheese's permanently closed. On November 26, an auction was held on-site, where all assets of the store, notably The King, were auctioned off. The King was purchased by a local fun center. His current whereabouts are unknown.

The portion of the plaza where the Pizza Time Theatre was located was demolished and rebuilt for a Lowe's, which remains today.
