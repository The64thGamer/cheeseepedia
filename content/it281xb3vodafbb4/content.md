***Greece, NY (3160 W Ridge Rd)*** was a Chuck E. Cheese's Pizza Time Theatre located within what is now Lowe's Plaza that opened on March 15, 1983, with a Balcony Stage and closed in November 1996 with a 2-Stage.

## History

The Greece Pizza Time Theatre opened on March 15, 1983. Its opening made it the second Pizza Time Theatre in the Rochester area after [Penfield, NY (1614 Penfield Rd)], as well as the sixth in New York. Greece's Balcony Stage probably opened with the [Tunnel of Love (Balcony Stage Theme)] that featured Madame Oink, as the store opened during the last month the theme was being used. If this was the case, Greece would have only had Madame Oink for about five months before replacing her with Helen Henny in August. The store would get the [Broadway (Balcony Stage Theme)] as a result of this. Greece also opened with The King, who was in the store for its entire lifetime. In 1989, the store would have its Balcony Stage retrofitted into a Chuck E.'s House Facade. In 1992, the store would receive its final stage, a 2-Stage.

## Closure and Fate of the Animatronics

In November 1996, the Greece Chuck E. Cheese's permanently closed. On November 26, an auction was held on-site, where all assets of the store, notably The King, were auctioned off. The King was purchased by a local fun center. His current whereabouts are unknown.

The portion of the plaza where the Pizza Time Theatre was located was demolished and rebuilt for a Lowe's, which remains today.

On July 5, 2003, [Greece, NY (3130 W Ridge Rd)] would open in the same plaza.
