***Greece, NY (3160 W Ridge Rd)*** was a Chuck E. Cheese's Pizza Time Theatre located within what is now Lowe's Plaza that opened on March 15, 1983, with a Balcony Stage and closed in November 1996 with a 2-Stage.

## History

The Greece Pizza Time Theatre opened on March 15, 1983.

## Closure and Fate of the Animatronics

In November 1996, the Greece Chuck E. Cheese's permanently closed. On November 26, an auction was held on-site, where all assets of the store, notably The King, were auctioned off. The King was purchased by a local fun center. His current whereabouts are unknown.

The portion of the plaza where the Pizza Time Theatre was located was demolished and rebuilt for a Lowe's.
