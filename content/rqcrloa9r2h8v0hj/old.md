***Lancaster, CA (44650 Valley Central Way)*** was a *Chuck E. Cheese's* that open on March 8, 1993 with a [1-Stage] and closed on March 11, 2011. The store relocated to Lancaster, CA (44410 Valley Central Way), which is still open to this day.
