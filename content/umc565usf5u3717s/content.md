20 years. $175,000. One incredible community.
 
This month, our 20th Annual Chuck E. Cheese Charity Golf Tournament raised a record $175,000 - and every dollar goes directly back to our people and the communities we serve.🏌️
 
The CEC Entertainment Foundation exists for moments when life gets hard. Emergency relief when the unexpected hits. Scholarships for Chuck E. Cheese and Peter Piper Pizza team members building a better future. Grants for the community organizations that support the kids and families at the heart of what we do.
 
Here's what that looks like in action:
 
🟢 CEC Cares Employee Relief Fund — Over $519,000 distributed across 303 employees in need since 2020.
 
🟢 CEC Opportunity Scholarship Fund — Over $135,000 awarded to employees, including 100+ scholarship payments to students on our team.
 
🟢 CEC Entertainment Foundation Fund — Over $59,000 in grants awarded to national and community organizations since 2021.
 
In 2025 alone, the Foundation distributed nearly $80,000 directly to our people and communities. This year's record fundraiser ensures we can do even more!
 
This incredible tradition is only possible through the generosity of our partners, sponsors, and the many employees who contribute a portion of every paycheck to the Foundation because they believe in taking care of one another. That's not a program - that's who we are. 🏆
