***Johnson City, NY (365 Harry L Dr)*** was a Chuck E. Cheese's Pizza Time Theatre located in the Small Mall that opened on June 7, 1983, with a Balcony Stage and closed on November 17, 1996, with a 2-Stage.

## History

The Johnson City Pizza Time Theatre opened on June 7, 1983, as the 247th Pizza Time Theatre to ever open. The store's manager was Rick Malloy, and its Lounge act was the Beach Bowzers. [1] The store also opened with the Fantasy Forest arcade. By April 1984, Johnson City had received Cheese E. Street (advertised as "Chuck E. Street" [(2)](#cite-1)), and was one of only two stores in New York to have it, the other being [Cheektowaga, NY (Thruway Mall)](/?v=cep-js&=7sl3l7rs7436q5u6). Notably, the store kept the PTT signage for the rest of its operation. In 1989, the store's Balcony Stage was retrofitted into a Chuck E.'s House Facade, which was replaced by a 2-Stage in around 1993.

## Closure

On November 17, 1996, the Johnson City Chuck E. Cheese's permanently closed. As of July 2023, the site is now multiple businesses.
