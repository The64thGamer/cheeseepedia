***Henrietta, NY (3400 W Henrietta Rd)*** was a Gadgets located at The Marketplace Mall that opened on May 4, 1983, with The Looney Tunes Revue and closed on January 27, 1985.

## History

### Opening

The Henrietta Gadgets opened on May 4, 1983, at 11:00am at The Marketplace Mall, and was located next to Sears. Like all Gadgets restaurants, the Henrietta restaurant was owned and operated by Leisure Development Corporation, a subsidiary of Warner Communications, and was the fifth of twelve Gadgets restaurants to ever open. The restaurant employed around 125 employees at the time of its opening. [1]

### Years of Operation

On June 11, 1983, around 200 unemployed individuals showed up at the Henrietta Gadgets to audition to be one of 40 extras for a 10-minute promotional training movie for Gadgets employees, with the restaurant promising to pay the current rate for film extras. However, the restaurant allegedly did not follow through on its "'gesture of good will to the community'" after the tryouts [2].

On October 29, 1983, the restaurant held a Halloween costume contest; the prize for best couple was a free 4-day vacation to the Virgin Islands, the prize for best individual was an arcade video game, and the prize for best group was $100 in cash [3].

### Closure

On January 27, 1985, the Henrietta Gadgets permanently closed [5], along with nine other restaurants due to poor sales and the dissolution of Leisure Development [6]. Though the space has had multiple tenants over the decades, it is currently vacant.

The Marketplace Mall closed on December 31, 2025.

### Fate of the Animatronics

Currently, the fates of the Henrietta Sammy Sands and Looney Tunes Revue are unknown.
