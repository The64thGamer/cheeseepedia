***Irving, TX (1707 Market Pl Blvd 200)*** is the Chuck E. Cheese corporate offices used by CEC Entertainment and the CEC Support Center from July of 2015 to June of 2026.

## History
They moved into the building in July 2015, after moving from the ShowBiz Pizza Time Corporate Office at [Irving, TX (4441 W Airport Fwy Ste 215)].
