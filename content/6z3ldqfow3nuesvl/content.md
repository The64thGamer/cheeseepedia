Chuck E. Cheese is thrilled to announce our recognition from DFW Child for Best Party Venue for Younger Kids in Dallas and Tarrant County! Being recognized by the community we love serving makes this award extra special.

From the decorations to the games to the birthday song, every detail matters — and this recognition is a reflection of the team members who bring that magic to life every single day.

We know how much birthdays mean to families. In a survey of nearly 1,900 parents, 51% say lighting the candles on the cake is the number one "proof-of-success" moment at their child's birthday. That number jumps to 76% for kids ages 6 and 7, when children are most aware of its social significance. That singular moment is something we take seriously, and something we deliver exceptionally every single day. Add to that the fact that 69% of parents say safety is a top priority, and 77% say making sure their child feels centered is another — and you will understand why families keep coming back to us.

That is why we put just as much intention into the first 60 seconds of every visit. From the moment a birthday child walks through our doors, we make sure they feel like the star of the show — because we know that first impression sets the tone for everything that follows.

From affordable packages for every budget, to live shows with Chuck E. that guarantee the brightest birthday smiles, to our newest Sensory Sensitive Birthdays designed to make every child's celebration feel just right for them, we are proud to be the home of birthdays that truly matter.

Thank you to DFWChild and our DFW families for your continued support and for trusting us to create fun-filled memories with every visit. We promise to keep the fun coming! 🎂🎈
