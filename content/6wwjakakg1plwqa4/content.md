***Sandy, UT (10535 So. State St)*** (Store #3203) was a franchised *Chuck E. Cheese's* location that opened in Summer of 1996 with a 1-Stage and closed in Fall of 2014. 

## History
During construction, the [Riverside, CA (4033 Chicago Ave)] would send its Cyberamic characters to the location for use on its 1-Stage show.

The store would open some time in early-mid 1996, next to the Brunswick Sandy Lanes[13], with its first known listing being on June 26, 1996 for an Assistant Manager position. A September 16th, 1996 coupon ad would note the store as the 'New' Chuck E. Cheese's.

At some point the store would receive [Phase 3 (2000)], and have their [Skytubes] relocated. In September 2011 the location would recieve the [CEC 2000's Remodel Program]. A percentage of the old art would remain, however, including the showroom shelves and walls. 

### Closure
By the Fall of 2014, the location closed and was turned into an expansion of the next-door All-Star Bowling franchise. The Cyberamics would then be sent to the [Orem, UT (40 E Center St)] store for parts on their 3-Stage show.

During All-Star Bowling's takeover of the space, the stage was cleared of everything, and a large projector screen was put on the stage. All of the walls were repainted, and all CEC branding would be removed, although various games would be reused, most notably the [Skytubes].[7] The Showroom, stage, [Artifact Shelves], signage, booths, and the hanging lights remained. The store would remain an All-Star Bowling until May 10th, 2026 where it would be permanently closed.[1][2]
