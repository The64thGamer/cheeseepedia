***Chuck E. Cheese's Show 4 2019* was** released on September 9, 2019, and played until November 3, 2019. This show featured two new songs alongside two reused from the previous year, as well as a new Halloween live show.

## Trivia:

* 'Pumpkin Patch' and 'I Didn't Need To Be Afraid' are originally from [Chuck E. Cheese's Show 6 2018].
* This show includes a new YouTube promo, this time just a 30-second video showcasing the main channel page and what type of content is uploaded, narrated by Matt Daniel.
* This show includes the standard version of Happy Dance instead of the Halloween remix. 
* This showtape was released on two discs for Studio C. Disc 1 includes the main show, while Disc 2 includes Diagnostics and Live Shows.
