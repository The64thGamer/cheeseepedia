Fats Abdomino was created for the [Wolf Pack 5] show and debuted at the 1978 IAAPA. He is a parody of rhythm and blues musician, Fats Domino. His mech was later reused for Billy Bill in the [Hard Luck Bears]. He would be renamed to Fats Gorilla in Showbiz Pizza Place

## Design

Fats is a gorilla that plays an upright piano. He wore a sparkling tuxedo with varying colors depending on location, a bow tie, and white undershirt. His design would be reused with little changes for [Fatz Geronimo (Animatronic)]
