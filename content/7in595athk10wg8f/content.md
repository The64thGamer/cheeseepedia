To celebrate Teacher Appreciation Week, we’re proud to honor the incredible educators who make a difference every day by offering a free 7” one-topping pizza to all teachers from May 4 - 8 at participating Arizona and Albuquerque locations. This offer is available from 3 - 7 p.m. and can be redeemed with a valid school ID on dine-in and carryout orders.

From STEM programs and local school partnerships to fundraisers, supporting children’s education has always been part of what we do at Peter Piper Pizza. 

Thank you to all the teachers for your dedication, care and the lasting impact you make in your classrooms and communities.
