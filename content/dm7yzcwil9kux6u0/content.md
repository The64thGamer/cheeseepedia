This showtape is surely a decent show. Maybe a little bit lacking but decent for 1982 standards. I mainly enjoy the way the show is all formatted, especially since the characters on stage announce the next show which this is the earliest known show to do this. The GOFA skit was pretty nice and the shows that came out of this tape were pretty good! A majority of these shows were also cross-released with GOFA and some of these shows even made it to compilation shows later on. However some of the content in this show were pretty lacking like the Crying Skit or Dragstrip in the Sky. Other than that, pretty okay show!
