***Pac-Man*** is an arcade game developed by Namco (now Bandai Namco). Originally released in Japan on May 22nd, 1980, it was exported to America starting that October and quickly became a massive success there.[1] Bally Midway obtained the rights to distribute it stateside.[2]

# Gameplay

*Pac-Man* is a maze game where the player controls the titular character (essentially a yellow circle with a mouth). Their goal is to eat all the dots ("pellets") in each level while avoiding four ghosts (or "monsters"). Pac-Man's only defense is eating one of four large dots ("Power Pellets") in the maze's corners, which will temporarily scare off the ghosts and make them edible. Eating them will send them back to the center, where they will regenerate and leave to continue roaming around. The ghosts switch between "scattering" (staying in one corner of the maze) and "chasing" down Pac-Man at set intervals. In addition, each ghost has unique AI patterns during "chase" mode: Blinky (red) always targets Pac-Man directly, Pinky (pink) tries to get in front of him, Inky (cyan)'s target depends on the position of both Pac-Man and Blinky, and Clyde (orange) normally targets Pac-Man but flees to his designated corner if he gets too close.[3] Various fruits also spawn in the middle of the maze, awarding bonus points when eaten.
