March is Women’s History Month, a time to celebrate the achievements, leadership, and impact of women across industries and communities.

At CEC Entertainment, women represent 53% of the leadership positions within our management teams, reflecting a culture that fosters growth and opportunity for all. We are inspired by the women across our brands who innovate, lead teams, serve guests, and help shape the future of family entertainment. Their contributions strengthen our organization and the communities we serve.

This month, we encourage reflection, learning, and continued advocacy for opportunity and advancement. Progress happens when diverse voices are heard and supported.

We’re proud to celebrate Women’s History Month and the leaders, past, present, and future, who move us forward.
