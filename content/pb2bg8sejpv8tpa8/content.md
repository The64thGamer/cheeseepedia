We’re proud to announce the promotion of Gillian Luo, CPA, to Vice President and Controller at CEC Entertainment.
 
Since joining CEC Entertainment in 2018, Gillian has continued to grow her impact and leadership across our Finance organization, most recently serving as Assistant Controller. Along the way, she has become a trusted cross-functional partner, strengthened our accounting processes and controls, and demonstrated a meaningful commitment to developing talent and her teams.
 
With 15 years of accounting experience, including five years with Deloitte, Gillian brings deep technical expertise, a strong understanding of our business and a passion for building high-performing teams.
 
Please join us in congratulating Gillian Luo on this well-earned next chapter!
