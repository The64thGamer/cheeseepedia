## **Characters**

### **Captain Andy**

Captain Andy was the Volunteer Fireman Chief, the leader of the show, and is who the show is named after. He was a schnauzer who wore a blue suit with a red and white undershirt and tie and a tall Breton hat, and played the banjo. He was voiced by **Michael McGinnis**, a singer-songwriter, musician, actor, and storyteller, and named after Andrew J. Whalen. [7]

### **Matthew**

Matthew was Captain Andy's "Right-hand Man" and basically the comic relief. He was a Seagull who wore a sailor-style hat. He was voiced by **Bruce Belland**.

### **Clarabel and Sparky**

Clarabel was a musical fire truck.

Sparky was a small dalmation that was sat on clarabel and would bark occasionally. Aperantly he was a last-minute addon to the show while the first clarabel was being built. He was also a fan favorite with the kids and had a plush made of him.

### **Gus Gus**

Gus Gus was a mute piano-playing dog who was mostly a background character yet was a part of the fireman band. Apparently, he was loosely based on Ralph Canard.

### **Goober, Happy, and Homer**

Goober, (Gus Gus), Happy, and Homer made up the volunteer fireman band. 

Happy was a fiddle-playing dog who had a high voice. Note that he was a favorite of the kids and based on Andrew Whalen's brother, who was nicknamed Happy. 

Homer was a dopey guitar-playing dog who had a low voice. He would always mess up the lyrics, and Happy and Goober would always correct him. 

Goober was a Gutbucket-playing dog who had a "going through puberty"-sounding voice. 

Both Goober and Homer were kind of carry-overs from some of Tom Reidenbach's work with Silver Dollar City. The band of beagles would come out of a big red firehouse that had a sliding door.
