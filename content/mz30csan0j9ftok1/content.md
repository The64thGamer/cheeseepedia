So much excitement and momentum for Chuck E. Cheese across Mexico!

I had the opportunity to spend the past few weeks traveling throughout Mexico, seeing how the Chuck E. Cheese brand continues to evolve, innovate, and create new family experiences.

Starting in Monterrey, where I had the chance to see the finishing touches on the new Chuck E. Cheese Adventure Zone attraction at our San Agustin location, which celebrated its grand opening this past weekend.

Next was Guadalajara, where we toured the all-new Chuck E. Cheese Adventure World concept, preparing for its grand opening this upcoming weekend. Another exciting example of how we continue to reinvent the family entertainment experience.

From there, it was on to Mexico City, visiting several of our locations that were fully transformed for our seasonal Chuck E. Cheese Boo-tacular celebration. It was great to see the creativity, energy, and enthusiasm our teams brought to the guest experience.

The capstone of the trip was the opportunity to speak at the Retail Expansion Summit sharing how Chuck E. Cheese continues to evolve as an attractive option for both new retail developments and existing retail spaces. With our increasingly flexible formats, we are creating new opportunities to bring the brand to more markets.

A special thank you to our incredible teams across Mexico, Emilio Rabago, Miguel Brunell, Francisco Cantú, Sofia Salcido, Fernando Leal, and Jennifer Guerrero for their dedication, passion, and commitment to our guests. And thank you to Jorge Lizan for the invitation to speak at such an outstanding conference, bringing together retail leaders not only from across Latin America, but from around the world.
