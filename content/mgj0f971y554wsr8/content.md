CEC Entertainment warmly recognizes Lunar New Year as the beginning of a new year on the lunisolar calendar. This holiday finds its importance in Chinese, Korean, and Vietnamese communities around the globe and marks a period of renewal, family, and good fortune.

Across CEC Entertainment, including Chuck E. Cheese and Peter Piper Pizza, we value the cultures and traditions that make our teams and communities so vibrant. During this time, we encourage you to explore diverse traditions outside of your own and understand the importance of these holidays to the communities they are celebrated in.
