***Richmond, VA (10430 Midlothian Tpke)***, Store #652, is a Chuck E. Cheese that opened on May 1, 1990, as the last ShowBiz Pizza Place to ever open. It received Concept Unification in fall 1992, and is still open with the 2.0 remodel. This location should not be confused with any of the other Richmond, VA locations.
