 The Looney Tunes Revue was created in 1981 for the Gadgets restaurants. At the first ever Gadgets location (called Gizmos) there was a prototype version of the show. The prototype show had 3 stages, and each stage had a character in the center. The stage in the middle was Daffy Duck playing a banjo. The stage on the right had a Porky Pig who played a guitar, and the one on the left had Bugs Bunny playing a piano. The characters outfits were unique to this stage, and Daffy was the only character to keep his instrument in future stage formats. The prototype show also came with a rough Yosemite Sam animatronic on a rotating stage near the front of the store. He would be able to spin around and lure people into the restaurant when he wasn't already performing inside.

 After the initial restaurant's success, Advanced Animations created the expanded version of the show. The expanded show laid the foundation that the future show variations would rely on. It had 3 stages, and way more characters. Bugs was back, and this time he was on the center stage. He was placed in the middle of the center stage. To the left side of the center stage was Porky Pig, who was now playing a clarinet. Yosemite returned, and he was placed on the right side of the center stage. The center stage had a raised platform in its back, and it housed 3 new characters. Pepe le Pew was playing a piano on the left of the platform, and on the right was Foghorn Leghorn playing a drum set. the Tasmanian Devil was playing a standing bass, and he was placed behind Bugs, on the center of the back platform. The center stage had 2 prop characters as well. In the front left and right were 2 trapdoors. Speedy Gonzales would rise from the left trapdoor and play maracas, and Henery Hawk would rise from the right trapdoor and play a tambourine. The main stage also had some automatically changing backdrops.
 The right stage had some more new characters. Sylvester played a horn, and on the far right was Sylvester Jr. Tweety was another semi-prop character, who would lower from the ceiling to sing from his cage. The left stage was left all for Daffy. Daffy has a special gimmick where an explosive would lower from the ceiling, bomb shelter doors would surround him, and his stage would secretly rotate. The real Daffy animatronics would turn around, and when the bomb doors opened, the audience would see an "Exploded Daffy".

 The expanded show would be placed in the first 2 main Gadgets locations, and after that they had to downsize. The reduced version of the show was installed in nearly every other Gadgets location that came after the first 2. The reduced show was primarily the same, the only difference being the removal of the Tasmanian Devil and Foghorn Leghorn. A few cosmetics were also altered, such as Bugs' mask.

 Even though every Gadgets had closed by the 90s, a few animatronics managed to survive. The last Looney Tunes Revue was converted into a "trailer show". This show would be driven around to various events, until it was eventually abandoned. This show would be found and distributed by random collectors for restoration. A Foghorn Leghorn animatronic from the programming show at Advanced Animations was found, meaning that the Tasmanian Devil is the only animatronic from the show to have been completely lost.

 Before Gadgets' downfall, there were also concepts for a new Looney Tunes Revue. Information about it is still a little muddy, but the main idea behind it would be that the audience would watch the animatronics perform live cartoons and tell stories.

 A large percent of this show's information has become lost media, and all that is left is whatever has trickled out of collector related social media.
