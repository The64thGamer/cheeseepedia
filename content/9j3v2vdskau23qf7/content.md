Today, on World Autism Awareness Day—and throughout the year—we’re proud to support inclusive, welcoming experiences for every guest who walks through our doors.

During World Autism Month, Chuck E. Cheese is celebrating the autism community through two Sensory Sensitive Sundays events, fundraising for our longtime partner, Autism Speaks, and the launch of NEW Sensory Sensitive Birthdays—an inclusive celebration designed to give families more flexibility based on their child’s needs.

We’re grateful to our teams and communities who help bring these experiences to life and continue to foster understanding and inclusion every day.
