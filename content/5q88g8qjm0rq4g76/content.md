***La Habra, CA (901 Harbor Blvd)*** was a [Pizza Time Theatre] that opened in May of 1980 with a [Portrait / Shelf Stage] before closing in 1984.

## History

### Pre-Opening

The first mention of La Habra would be in December of 1979 as a soon to open store alongside Covina, Fullerton, and Sun Valley.[5] Ads for an assistant manager would appear on April 13th.[2] With the May issue of The Pizza Times, it would be noted La Habra was set to open "...any day now".[6] Assistant manager of [Huntington Beach, CA (15511 Edwards St)], Joe Fisher, would train the kitchen staff for both [Fullerton, CA (1841 W Orangethorpe Ave)] and La Habra, while being set to be part of the opening staff for the store. [Stockton, CA (4555 N Pershing Ave)] would also transfer the manager John Eliasen to La Habra. [7]

### Operation

In early 1981, the store, [Huntington Beach, CA (15511 Edwards St)], [Fullerton, CA (1841 W Orangethorpe Ave)], and [Garden Grove, CA (11889 Valley View St)] would participate in the "Pizza Leagues" where softball players at each store would compete. These four stores and the [Long Beach, CA (4706 Los Coyotes Diagonal)] location would also sponsor California Surfs soccer team's MVP award.[7] From August 24th to September 3rd, all stores would participate in the [1981 Play Asteroids and Benefit Muscular Dystrophy (Event)].[8]

### Closure

The store would close some time in early to mid 1984, with sale of the location set in October.[3] The auction of the final items would happen in December. [1]
