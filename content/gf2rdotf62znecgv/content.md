***???, Japan*** was a planned [Chuck E. Cheese's] that would have been set to open in 1999 somewhere in Japan.[1]

## History

In early February of 1998, Nihon Keizai Shimbun would publish a currently lost article that would be summarized in an Ad Age blurb on February 27, 1998. [2] The article would state that ShowBiz Pizza Time had plans to open a [Chuck E. Cheese's] in Japan through a partnership with a Japanese franchisee, which was still in the process of being selected "...among Japanese restaurant operators, retailers and trading companies.". The expectations would be that the first location would likely open in early 1999 with the current plans. 

Nothing is noted about the location other than it offering arcades and food samplings, with it unclear if these would be similar to the American branch or localized, likely undecided at this point in the process. Ultimately, no other reports regarding any selected franchisee would release, and a location would never open. Given the time period, the planned store would have likely opened with the [SPT 1990's Remodel Program] and a [Studio C Alpha].
