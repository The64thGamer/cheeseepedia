***Harmony II 'Harmony's Hoedown'*** is the second and final Pizza Time Theatre Showtape featuring the guest star *Harmony Howlette. This show would play for six months following a six-month run of [Harmony Howlette's Wild West Show]. Both Harmony shows would be played for six months each at new stores opening through April 1982. ## Trivia

* Chuck E.'s cousin Buster of Bronx (the original buster of broncs), who was the subject of a song in [Harmony Howlette's Wild West Show], is mentioned in segment 4.
* Several skits in this show remake songs and dialogue from previous showtapes. Segment 6 is a remake of 'Jasper's Texas Show', which originates from a showtape at the Winchester location and later reused in [Around the World with Madam Oink]. Segment 7 features a shortened version of 'Chuck E. Cheese's Theme Song' from various showtapes. Segment 9 is a partial remake of 'Smile Jasper Smile', another skit that originates from Winchester. 'Side by Side' is performed in Segment 10, with dialogue originating from the Finale Skit used in various early showtapes. Segment 11 is a remake of 'Lone Prairie', a segment from an unknown 70s showtape.
