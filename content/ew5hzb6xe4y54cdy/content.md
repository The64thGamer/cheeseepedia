The ***PTT 1980 Press Kit*** was a set of documents and photos released to news associations around 1980.[3] It contains promotional photos and flyers seen in other releases, though it is unknown if there are other variants of this press kit from earlier or later years.[1]

## Documents

The set of documents are wrapped in a folder containing PTT art from 1979, the PTT logo, and the title "PRESS KIT".

## History

The kit would release to journalists and newsrooms sometime around May of 1980.[3] The kit would first be directly found through an eBay listing on October 7th, 2026 from a seller in Los Angeles. The description stated:

> My father used to work as a journalist and got this press kit during his time reviewing movies. I'm not sure how to verify that it's complete but I believe it to be, so I'm including photos of everything that comes with it. Want it to find a good home with someone who will appreciate it.

No other versions of the press kit have surfaced.
