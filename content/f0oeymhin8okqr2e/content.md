***Greece, NY (3130 W Ridge Rd)*** (also known as ***Rochester***) is a Chuck E. Cheese's located within Lowe's Plaza that opened on July 5, 2003, with a Studio C Beta and remains open with a Dance Floor and Video Wall. This location should not be confused with [Greece, NY (3160 W Ridge Rd)], which was a Pizza Time Theatre that opened on March 15, 1983, with a Balcony Stage and closed in November 1996 with a 2-Stage. It also should not be confused with [Henrietta, NY (1000 Hylan Drive)], which was a Chuck E. Cheese's that opened on June 30, 2001, with a Studio C Alpha and closed on June 25, 2020, and was also occasionally known as "Rochester."

## History

The Greece Chuck E. Cheese's opened on July 5, 2003, with a Studio C Beta with a [Chuck E. Cheese 16m (Animatronic)]. The store opened in the same plaza where the original Greece location was located. The store opened with [Phase 3 (2000)], but would receive the [CEC 2000's Remodel Program] not long after. The store would remain like this until 2018, when it would receive the [CEC 2.0 Remodel Program], which removed the Studio C Beta and its 16m bot and replaced it with a Dance Floor. This store was the third to receive the 2.0 remodel in New York. Greece did not receive a Video Wall until around 2024/2025.

## Present Day

The Greece store remains open with the 2.0 remodel. Since the closure of Henrietta, it is currently the only Chuck E. Cheese's in the Rochester area.
