After 28 years of dedication to Peter Piper Pizza, we are celebrating the retirement of Donald “Don” Pijut. 🍕

Don joined Peter Piper Pizza in 1984 as a Training Director and went on to leave an enduring mark across multiple chapters of our history. From helping shape iconic menu items like the Chicago Classic and New York 3 Cheese pizzas to leading memorable campaigns such as the Battle of the Pizzas, Don’s influence lives on in ways our Guests still enjoy today.

One of Don’s favorite memories was working alongside our founder, Tony Cavolo. With his striped apron and chef’s hat, Tony could film 10 to 15 TV spots in just a few hours, always finishing with his signature arm wave and “C’mon over to Peter Piper Pizza.” 

He became such a recognizable personality that people would stop him on the street just to hear the line again, New York accent included.

Through ownership changes, evolving logos and new chapters for the brand, one thing never changed. Don’s belief that Peter Piper Pizza is not just in the pizza business, but in the people business. That belief helped shape who we are today.

Wishing Don Pijut a happy and well-deserved retirement filled with travel, time with his grandkids and plenty of Chicago Classic pizza on thin crust, his favorite. 💛
