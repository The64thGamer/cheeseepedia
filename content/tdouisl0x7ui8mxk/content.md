This February, CEC Entertainment proudly recognizes Black History Month, a time to celebrate the richness and vitality of the Black community. During this time and beyond, we encourage you to become more familiar with some of the Black changemakers that continue to enrich our larger society.
 
The vibrancy of this community comes in many forms—be it podcasts like "Dreams of Black Wall Street," literature by authors like Bell Hooks and Zora Neale Hurston, or cinema like "Harriet" and "One Night in Miami." Exploring diverse perspectives helps foster empathy and understanding of communities outside of our own, values that are vital to our culture at CEC.

As we look ahead, we remain committed to an environment grounded in excellence, inclusion, and celebration, this month and every month! Happy Black History Month!
