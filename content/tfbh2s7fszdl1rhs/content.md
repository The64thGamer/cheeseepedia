***Hamburg, NY (4408 Milestrip Rd)***, Store #516 (also known as ***Buffalo***, ***Blasdell***, or, erroneously, ***Orchard Park***), is a corporate-owned Chuck E. Cheese's located within BJ's Plaza that opened on May 31, 2000, with a Studio C Alpha and is still open with the 2.0 remodel. This location should not be confused with [Buffalo (North Buffalo), NY (2638 Delaware Ave)], which is a Chuck E. Cheese that opened on November 22, 2024.

## History

### Discovery Zone

The Hamburg Discovery Zone opened in January 1994, and was the first of three that operated in the Buffalo area. All three operated until 1999, when they closed as a result of Discovery Zone's bankruptcy.

In late 1999, CEC purchased 13,400 square feet of the 18,000 square-foot former Hamburg Discovery Zone, while the remaining 4,600 square feet was purchased by the children's retailer Priceless Kids. [1]

### Chuck E. Cheese's

#### Opening

The Hamburg Chuck E. Cheese's opened on May 31, 2000, with a Studio C Alpha with a 32-movement bot, a blue screen [3], a camera [3], and an interactive console [3]. From 2004/05 to, at the latest, 2012, Chuck donned his Cool outfit, which was replaced by his Avenger outfit [2], which he wore until the stage was removed.

#### Expansion

In June 2006, Priceless Kids permanently closed due to the phasing out of the Priceless Kids name and the company's rebranding to 4 Ever Kids. Following this, Chuck E. Cheese's expanded into the former store space and used the area as a new game area, knocking down the separation wall. Additionally, a wheelchair-accessible ramp and three stairs leading to the new game room were installed.

#### 2010s

In 2013/14, the blue screen, camera, and interactive console were removed from the stage. In 2014, the SkyTubes were removed and were replaced by Chuck E. Cheese's Fun House [5]. In late 2017, the stage curtains stopped functioning, as well as Chuck's eyelids and foot tap.

#### Pre-2.0

In 2020, Hamburg briefly closed due to the COVID-19 pandemic. That same year, Chuck E. Cheese's Fun House was removed from the store.

#### 2.0 Remodel

Hamburg began its 2.0 remodel on July 20, 2022 [6]. Its initial start date was back in December of the previous year, however, it was continuously pushed back, first from December to March, then from March to May, and finally from May to July. August 1, 2022, was the last operating day of the Studio C Alpha, and the next day, Chuck was removed and the curtains were closed; by August 4, the entire stage had been removed, and the Dance Floor had been fully installed and lit [6]. Instead of the standard 2.0 remodel, Hamburg was granted the "signature store" title and was given the signature 2.0 remodel package. This included everything that a standard 2.0 would get, as well as the addition of a custom mural featuring the characters exploring Niagara Falls [4]. With this remodel, Hamburg would see its showroom area and kiddie area swap places, in favor of having the giant video wall visible from outside the building.

#### Present Day

The Hamburg Chuck E. Cheese is currently one of two in the Buffalo area, and is the flagship store for the area. [4]
