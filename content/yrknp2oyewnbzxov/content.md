CEC Entertainment is pleased to congratulate Scott Drake as our new President and Chief Executive Officer! 

Drake succeeds David McKillips, who served as CEO from 2020 to early 2026, leading the organization through some of the most challenging moments the brands had experienced in recent history. From managing the pandemic and macro-economic challenges across the entire industry to spearheading progressive initiatives that positioned the brands for growth and continued success. 

This next chapter in leadership will be characterized by Drake’s sharp analytic insight, invaluable leadership, and his forward-thinking mindset. With 30 years of experience in transformational roles and a background in Strategy, Efficiency, Collaboration and Finance, Scott brings the expertise and vision to drive continued growth across our portfolio. 

We extend our sincere thanks to David for his leadership and lasting impact, and we look forward to the contributions Scott will make as he leads CEC Entertainment into the future. 

Congratulations, Scott! We’re excited to see the impact you’ll make!
