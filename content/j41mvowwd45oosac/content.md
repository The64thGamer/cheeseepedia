We’re proud to continue our #SliceOfKnowledge campaign!

In partnership with Read Better Be Better, an Arizona-based nonprofit dedicated to helping young students build a lifelong love of reading, every visit to Peter Piper Pizza becomes an opportunity to support early literacy.

Through April 26, guests at Phoenix, Tucson and Albuquerque locations can donate $2 at the register and receive five coupons for free 12-ounce ICEEs. Every dollar raised will go directly toward the Slice of Knowledge Foundation, benefiting Read Better Be Better’s literacy and leadership programs.

This campaign is about bringing families together and giving back in a meaningful way. Stop by, bring your family and be part of helping Arizona students succeed!
